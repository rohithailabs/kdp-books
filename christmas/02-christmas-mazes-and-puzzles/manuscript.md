# Christmas Mazes & Puzzles for Kids
## Manuscript — Page-by-Page Content Plan

Format: mixed holiday activity book — mazes, dot-to-dot, word searches, spot-the-difference, count-and-match, I-spy, and a few coloring pages. Difficulty increases through the book (easy → medium → hard) for ages 4–8. One activity per page, page numbers bottom-center on all interior activity/answer pages. Trim size 8.5" x 8.5".
