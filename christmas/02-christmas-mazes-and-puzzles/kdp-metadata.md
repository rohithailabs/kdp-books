### Back-Cover / Product Description
Get ready for a holly-jolly brain workout! **Christmas Mazes & Puzzles for Kids** is packed with 35 festive activities that grow trickier page by page, so kids ages 4 to 8 can start easy and build up their puzzle-solving confidence all the way to Christmas Eve.

Inside, your child will help Rudolph find Santa's sleigh through a snowy maze, hunt for hidden objects in Santa's workshop, circle sneaky differences between two gingerbread houses, search for holiday words hidden in letter grids, count candy canes and match them to numbers, connect the dots to reveal a Christmas tree or a jingling bell, and even take on "The Great Christmas Eve Maze" — the biggest challenge in the book!

A handful of simple coloring pages are sprinkled in for a relaxing break between puzzles, and every maze, word search, and spot-the-difference page has its answer waiting in the answer key at the back — no frustration, just fun. Each activity fills its own full 8.5" x 8.5" page, with bold, easy-to-see lines made just for young hands holding a pencil or crayon.

With a "This Book Belongs To" page, a simple parent note, a handy "what's inside" key, and a "Great Job!" certificate to celebrate finishing the whole book, this is a screen-free way to keep kids engaged during holiday travel, classroom parties, or cozy nights by the tree.

Give your favorite 4-8 year old hours of holiday puzzle fun — grab your copy today!
