# Christmas Mazes & Puzzles for Kids
## Illustration Briefs

### Recurring Style Guide (applies to every interior page)

- **Line art only:** pure black line art on white, no gray fills, no shading, no color (except that coloring pages are, by definition, left open for the child to fill). Print-ready at 300 DPI, black ink only.
- **Line weight:** bold outer contours (~3pt equivalent) for characters/objects; maze walls and puzzle-grid lines drawn at a consistent ~2.5pt weight so they read clearly at 8.5"x8.5" trim; thinner ~1pt guide lines for dotted paths, grid rules, and small interior details.
- **Character/prop style:** same rounded, friendly, big-eyed character design used across the whole Christmas line (Santa, elves, Mrs. Claus, reindeer, animals) for visual consistency with the companion coloring and dot-to-dot books.
- **Maze construction:** single-solution mazes (one correct path from start to finish, clearly marked start flag/icon and end flag/icon). Corridor width and complexity scale with difficulty:
  - *Easy* (pages 5): wide corridors (~0.4"), 0–1 dead-end branch, short overall path.
  - *Medium* (pages 11, 17, 22): standard corridors (~0.3"), 2–4 dead-end branches, moderate path length.
  - *Hard* (pages 27, 31, 35): narrower corridors (~0.25"), 5+ dead-end branches/loops, longest paths, page 39 is the largest/most complex maze in the book (full-bleed safe-area maze).
  - Maze walls are bold continuous lines; background inside the maze stays white/open (no shading) so pencil tracing stays visible.
- **Dot-to-dot construction:** dots are small bold-outlined circles with the number printed just outside each dot (never overlapping the connecting line path), numeral font is a clean rounded sans-serif at a size readable by early readers. A faint (light gray in the source file, printed as a thin dashed line or omitted entirely and left to the child) preview is NOT shown — the finished shape is only implied by the dot positions plus the theme caption. Provide the completed reference art separately from the dot-only interior page for the illustrator's own tracking.
- **Word search grids:** bold-outline square grid, one capital letter per cell in a clean monospace-style font, word bank listed below the grid in the same bold rounded style as captions.
- **Spot-the-difference pairs:** two side-by-side (or stacked, given the square trim — side-by-side works better at 8.5"x8.5" if each panel is kept simple) versions of the same friendly scene, redrawn with the specified number of deliberate differences (added/removed/moved/resized small elements — never differences that would confuse a child, like flipping the whole image).
- **I Spy scenes:** single busy-but-friendly full scene with the listed hidden objects worked naturally into the composition (not obviously spotlighted) but still findable within 1–2 minutes by a 5–8 year old.
- **Count & Match:** simple rows/groups of a repeated small icon (ornament, candy cane, reindeer, present, snowflake) plus a column of bold numeral targets; leave a clear empty margin between groups and numbers for a drawn matching line.
- **Answer key thumbnails:** small (roughly 2"x2") simplified reproductions of each maze/word search/spot-the-difference page with the solution marked in a bold dashed line (mazes), circled letters (word search), or circled differences (spot the difference); each thumbnail labeled "Page X" in small bold type.
- **Inclusivity note:** vary skin tone/hair among any human characters across the book.
- **Trim size:** 8.5" x 8.5" page, safe area approx. 7.5" x 7.5", 0.5"+ inner margin kept clear of maze walls/grid content for binding.

### Page 5 — Maze (Easy): Help the Elf Find the Workshop
Small elf figure at top-left as the start icon, workshop door with a wreath as the end icon bottom-right. One clear winding path, wide corridors, a single short dead-end branch for a gentle first challenge. Light candy-cane/holly doodles outside the maze border only (not inside corridors).

### Page 6 — Coloring: O Christmas Tree!
Single centered decorated tree (matches style of companion coloring book tree page), open ornament/star shapes ready to color, title caption above.

### Page 7 — Count & Match: Count the Ornaments!
Three small identical pine-tree outlines in a row, each decorated with a different count of plain circle ornaments (2, 4, 6). Below/beside them, three large bold numerals (2, 4, 6) in scrambled order with blank space between tree row and numeral row for a drawn line.

### Page 8 — I Spy: Santa's Workshop
Wide workshop scene: workbenches, shelves, a wrapped-gift pile, toy parts scattered pleasantly (not chaotic). Hide 1 teddy bear, 2 wrapped gifts, 3 wooden blocks, and 1 pair of ice skates naturally among the toys and shelves.

### Page 9 — Dot-to-Dot (1–10): Star
10 dots arranged in a classic 5-point star outline, numbers 1–10 placed just outside each dot in order around the perimeter.

### Page 10 — Word Search (Easy): 6 Words
8x8 grid, letters sized large and legible; words TREE, STAR, SNOW, ELF, GIFT, BELL placed horizontally and vertically only, filler letters kept simple (avoid letters that could spell accidental words). Word bank listed below grid.

### Page 12 — Spot the Difference (Easy): Two Gingerbread Houses

Small numbered blank circles below each panel for the child to tally found differences (optional).
