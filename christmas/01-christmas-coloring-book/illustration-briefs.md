# Merry & Bright: My First Christmas Coloring Book
## Illustration Briefs

### Recurring Style Guide (applies to every interior page)

- **Line art only:** pure black line art on white, no gray fills, no shading, no color. All art must print cleanly at 300 DPI in black ink only.
- **Line weight:** bold outer contour lines (~3pt equivalent), slightly thinner inner detail lines (~1.5–2pt equivalent) so small children can stay inside the lines easily.
- **Detail level:** minimal-to-moderate. Large, open, simple shapes for single-object pages (ages 4–5); a bit more detail and 2–4 secondary elements for full-scene pages (ages 6–8), but never cluttered or "busy."
- **Character design:** rounded, friendly, big-eyed, chubby-cheeked character style throughout for visual consistency (Santa, elves, Mrs. Claus, reindeer, snowmen, animals). Keep proportions consistent across pages featuring the same character.
- **Composition:** subject centered or rule-of-thirds placed, with generous open background space (never edge-to-edge dense detail) so young colorists don't feel overwhelmed. Leave at least 0.5" clean margin on the binding (inner/left) edge of every page.
- **Text placement:** page title sits as simple bold hand-lettering-style text at the top of the page, then the illustration fills the remainder. Page number centered at the very bottom, small.
- **Inclusivity note:** vary the skin tone/hair of human characters (Santa's helpers, carolers, sledding kids, gingerbread people icing details, etc.) across pages so the cast reads as diverse, not identical.
- **No scary/dark elements:** everything is warm, cheerful, and bright — no shadows, no nighttime-horror lighting; snow and night scenes use simple stars/moon line icons only.
- **Trim size:** 8.5" x 8.5" page, illustration safe area approx. 7.5" x 7.5" centered, 0.5" bleed where art runs to the edge (front/back cover only — interior art should stay within the safe area).

### Page 4 — Santa Claus Waving
Single large Santa figure, waist-up or full body, one hand raised in a wave, other hand on his belt. Big open coat with plain buttons (open fill areas), simple boots, fluffy hat pom-pom and beard rendered as bold outlines with a few curl lines only (avoid dense texture). Centered composition, friendly open face.

### Page 5 — Rudolph the Red-Nosed Reindeer
Front-facing reindeer, standing, antlers symmetrical and simply branched. Nose is a plain closed circle outline (no shading) so kids can color it bright red/orange. Small jingle bell on a collar as an optional tiny accent.

### Page 6 — O Christmas Tree!
Classic triangular tree shape divided into 3–4 tiers with simple scalloped branch lines. 6–8 plain circle/star ornament outlines scattered on it, a star on top, 2 simple wrapped-present outlines at the base. Open background.

### Page 7 — Frosty Friend
Three-tier snowman (large, medium, small stacked circles), simple top hat, straight scarf with fringe lines, two dot-outline buttons, stick arms as simple lines with small mitten-tip circles.

### Page 8 — Gingerbread Smiles
Classic gingerbread-person silhouette, simple bold outline, 3 round icing button outlines down the front, icing zigzag trim on limbs, bow at the neck, round cheek circles.

### Page 9 — Stocking Stuffed with Cheer
Single large stocking shape with a folded cuff (fur-trim texture as simple scallop line), candy cane peeking out the top, a few small toy shapes (ball, small box) peeking near the opening.

### Page 10 — A Gift for You
Single square/rectangular present, one big bow with two looping ribbon tails, one ribbon line crossing the box vertically and horizontally, tiny gift tag outline.

### Page 11 — Santa's Little Helper
Single elf character, full body, pointed hat with pom-pom, pointed curled-toe shoes, wide belt, holding a wrapped present in both arms.

### Page 12 — Shake the Snow Globe
Round glass globe outline on a simple pedestal base, small evergreen tree inside, a scatter of small circle "snowflakes" inside and a couple drifting just above the globe.

### Page 13 — Sweet & Stripy
Single large candy cane (J-shape), simple diagonal stripe guide lines (kids color alternating stripes), bow tied at the bend.

### Page 14 — Merry Wreath
Circular wreath made of simple scalloped/leaf-cluster outlines, 4–5 round berry outlines spaced around it, one bow at the bottom.

### Page 15 — Cool Penguin
Single standing penguin, round belly, simple wing-flippers, wearing a long scarf with fringe ends trailing to one side.

### Page 16 — Snowman Family
Scene: three snowmen in a row (tall dad, medium mom with a bow, small kid with a smaller hat), simple snowy ground line, 2–3 background pine trees, a couple of falling-snow dot outlines in the sky.

### Page 17 — Up, Up, and Away!
Scene: Santa's sleigh in profile mid-flight, packed with a few simple gift-box outlines, pulled by 3–4 reindeer in a harness line, simple rooftop/chimney silhouettes below, a crescent moon and a few star outlines in the sky.

### Page 18 — Stockings Hung with Care
Scene: Santa kneeling beside a fireplace mantel, stockings hanging from simple hooks, a sack of toys beside him, a lit candle or simple wreath decoration on the mantel.

### Page 19 — Trim the Tree!
Scene: two children (different heights/hair) decorating a tree — one reaching up with an ornament, one holding a garland strand — a small box of ornaments on the floor, tree partly decorated (leave open areas for more ornaments to color in).

### Page 20 — Sing We Now
Scene: 3 caroling figures of varying heights standing in the snow, each holding a simple songbook, in front of a house with a wreath on the door and a lamppost beside them.

### Page 21 — Santa's Workshop
Scene: two elves at simple workbenches, one hammering a toy horse, one wrapping a box, shelves of simple toy-shape outlines in the background, a window showing snow outside.

### Page 22 — Baking with Mrs. Claus
Scene: Mrs. Claus in an apron pulling a tray of round cookie outlines from an oven, a mixing bowl and rolling pin on a counter nearby, simple checkered-curtain window in the background (leave checks as open squares).

### Page 23 — Cozy by the Fire
Scene: lit fireplace with 3 stockings hanging, a cat curled asleep on a rug in front, a garland with simple ornament dots along the mantel, an armchair to one side.

### Page 24 — Polar Bear in a Santa Hat
Single sitting polar bear, round friendly face, simple fur outline (no dense texture), wearing a Santa hat with pom-pom.

### Page 25 — Jingle All the Way
Single reindeer profile, head turned toward viewer, wearing a wide collar with 3–4 round bell outlines and small line "jingle" marks near the bells.

### Page 26 — Shiny Ornament
Single large round ornament, close-up, with a swirl or spiral line pattern across the front, a cap and ribbon loop at the top for hanging.

### Page 27 — Gingerbread House
Single small house with a steep icing-trimmed roof (scalloped edge), round candy shapes (simple circles) along the roofline, a door with a tiny wreath, 1–2 lollipop shapes "planted" beside it.

### Page 28 — Star on Top
Single 5-point star, simple radiating line rays around it (like a sparkle), small ribbon or string looping from one point as if it just came off the tree.

### Page 29 — Santa's Boot
Single tall boot in profile, fur-trim cuff (scallop line), simple buckle detail, oversized and centered for younger colorists.

### Page 30 — Cozy Mittens
Two mittens clipped to a string with a small clothespin outline, simple cuff-fold line and a small snowflake or heart stitched-pattern outline on each mitten front.

### Page 31 — Warm Cocoa
Single mug (simple curved handle), 3–4 rounded marshmallow outlines piled on top, a candy cane resting against the rim as a stirrer, a couple of small steam-swirl lines above.

### Page 32 — Snowflake Sparkle
Single large 6-point symmetrical snowflake made of simple branching lines and small circle accents at the tips — geometric and satisfying for detail-oriented older colorists.

### Page 33 — Puppy's First Sweater
Single sitting puppy (floppy ears, wagging tail line), wearing a sweater with a simple chevron or star pattern outline on the chest.

### Page 34 — Checking It Twice
Scene: Santa standing beside his sleigh holding a long scroll/list that unrolls to the ground, pencil in the other hand, sleigh and one reindeer visible behind him.

### Page 35 — A Snack for the Reindeer
Scene: 2 reindeer with heads down eating a small pile of carrot outlines in the snow, sleigh parked in the background, a simple picket fence line.

### Page 36 — Sled Down the Hill!
Scene: two kids on a single sled going downhill (motion lines behind the sled), pine trees flanking the hill, small snow-drift mounds in the foreground.

### Page 37 — Town Square Tree
Scene: a large decorated tree centered in a small town square, 2–3 simple house/shop silhouettes around it, a lamppost, a bench, and a light dusting of falling-snow dot outlines.

### Page 38 — Snowy Owl
Single owl perched on a snow-capped branch, large round eyes, simple feather-line texture (minimal), wearing a small knit scarf around its neck.

### Page 39 — All Aboard the Christmas Train
Single toy train (engine + one car) in profile, a wreath or holly sprig mounted on the front, small circular wheels, simple smoke-puff outline from the funnel.

### Page 40 — Angel Ornament
Single angel figure, simple flowing robe lines, two symmetrical wing shapes spread to the sides, a halo ring above the head, hands clasped.

### Page 41 — Ring the Bell
Single large bell shape, clapper visible at the bottom opening, a bow tied at the top with two ribbon tails, 2–3 small motion/sound lines beside it to suggest a ring.

### Page 42 — Toy Shelf
Scene: a wide shelf holding a simple drum, a rocking horse, a rag doll, and 3 stacked alphabet blocks, all lined up and ready to color individually.

### Page 43 — Merry Christmas, Everyone!
Grand finale scene: Santa and Mrs. Claus in the center, flanked by 2 reindeer on one side and 2–3 elves on the other, all waving, standing outside the workshop with the sleigh and a decorated tree in the background — the most detailed page in the book, reserved for confident colorists finishing the book.

### Certificate Page (Page 44)
Decorative border of alternating small ornaments, holly leaves, and stars framing the certificate text block; a ribbon-banner shape at the top for the "Great Job!" headline; open star or badge shape in one corner for extra flourish coloring.
