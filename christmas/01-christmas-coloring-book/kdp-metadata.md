### Back-Cover / Product Description
Deck the halls with a coloring book made just for little hands! **Merry & Bright: My First Christmas Coloring Book** is packed with 40 large, easy-to-color holiday pictures designed especially for kids ages 4 to 8.

Inside, your child will meet a waving Santa, Rudolph the Red-Nosed Reindeer, a family of cheerful snowmen, busy elves in the workshop, a cozy fireside scene, carolers in the snow, a shiny snow globe, a gingerbread house, and so many more festive favorites — one picture per page, printed with bold, simple lines that are perfect for crayons, markers, or colored pencils.

Early pages feature big, single-object pictures that are perfect for beginning colorists, while later pages grow into fuller holiday scenes for kids who are ready for a bit more detail. Every page is a full-size 8.5" x 8.5" black-and-white line drawing, so nothing shows through from the back — and pages are easy to remove and display or gift.

This book also includes a "This Book Belongs To" page for personalizing, a note for parents, and a "Great Job!" certificate at the end to celebrate finishing the whole book — making it a sweet keepsake as well as a fun activity.

Perfect for holiday travel, stocking stuffers, classroom parties, quiet-time activities, or a gift alongside a fresh box of crayons — **Merry & Bright** brings hours of calm, creative holiday cheer to your child's Christmas season.
