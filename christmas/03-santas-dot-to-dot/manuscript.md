# Santa's Dot-to-Dot Adventure
## Manuscript — Page-by-Page Content Plan

Format: dot-to-dot focused activity book. 22 themed connect-the-dots pages (mostly 1–20, with easier 1–10/1–15 pages mixed in for younger kids) plus front/back matter. Every dot-to-dot page reveals a picture, then invites the child to color it in. One activity per page, page numbers bottom-center on interior activity pages. Trim size 8.5" x 8.5".
