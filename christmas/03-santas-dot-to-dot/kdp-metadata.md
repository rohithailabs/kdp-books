### Back-Cover / Product Description
Follow the numbers all the way to Christmas! **Santa's Dot-to-Dot Adventure** invites kids ages 4 to 8 to connect the dots and watch 22 festive pictures magically appear — a Christmas tree, Santa's sleigh, a friendly reindeer, a cozy stocking, a wreath, a snow globe, and even jolly Santa's face himself, all revealed one number at a time.

The book starts gentle, with simple 1-to-10 pictures like a star and a candy cane, before building up to 1-to-15 and 1-to-20 pages that give growing counters a fun new challenge. A clear "How to Play" page walks little ones through the steps, and every finished picture comes with an invitation to color it in — turning each page into two activities in one: counting practice and creative coloring.

Every picture is a full 8.5" x 8.5" page with large, clearly numbered dots that are easy for young hands to follow with a pencil or crayon. It's a screen-free way to practice number order, build fine motor skills, and get into the holiday spirit, whether at home, in the car, or waiting for the big day to arrive.

With a "This Book Belongs To" page, a friendly note for grown-ups, and a "Great Job!" certificate to celebrate finishing the whole adventure, this book makes a wonderful stocking stuffer, classroom gift, or quiet holiday activity for the counters in your life.
