# Halloween Mazes & Puzzles for Kids — Illustration Briefs

## Recurring Style Guide (applies to every interior page)

- **Line art only** — pure black outlines on white, no gray fill, no shading, no color, except where a puzzle mechanically requires filled black cells (none in this book — word search letters and maze walls stay as outlined line art).
- **Line weight:** bold outer contour/wall lines at an equivalent of 3pt; inner detail lines at 2pt; puzzle-mechanic lines (maze walls, dot-to-dot numerals, grid lines) at a consistent 2.5pt so they stay clearly distinct from character art.
- **Character style:** same rounded, chubby "cute-spooky" proportions across the whole series — big heads, big friendly eyes, soft curves. No blood, no weapons, no genuinely scary expressions. Gravestones are rounded-top and plain (no scary epitaphs); skeletons are cartoon-smiling; mummies, vampires, and witches are all friendly-faced.
- **Maze construction rules:** every maze has exactly ONE correct solution path from start to finish. Walls are drawn as bold parallel double lines with generous corridor width (minimum equivalent of 0.4" at print size) so a young child's crayon or fat pencil can trace it. Start point marked with a clear arrow and/or character; end point marked with the destination object. Easy mazes (pages 4, 7, 12): no or one short dead-end branch. Medium mazes (pages 16, 22, 26): 2-4 dead-end branches. Hard mazes (pages 30, 37): 5-6 dead-end branches with more winding turns, but still solvable by the target age group without frustration — no crossing paths, no visually confusing overlaps.
- **Dot-to-dot construction rules:** dots are large filled circles (easily poked with a pencil tip) with the numeral printed just outside each dot, numerals large enough to read at a glance. Dots spaced far enough apart that connecting lines don't crowd. The hidden shape must be recognizable from a reasonable distance even before connecting (for the answer key thumbnail).
- **Word search construction rules:** letter grids use a clean monospace-style block letter font, one letter per cell, cells large enough for a young child to circle with a crayon (minimum 0.35" cell). Word list printed below or beside the grid in the same friendly lettering as page titles. No word ever wraps oddly or touches the grid edge without full containment.
- **Spot-the-difference construction rules:** the two panels are near-identical copies of the same base illustration; differences are large, clear, and object-level (an object added/removed, a face expression changed, a size changed) — never a one-pixel-style subtle shading change, since the interior is black-line only.
- **Composition:** every activity fills roughly 80% of the live print area, centered, with a title at the top in rounded hand-lettering-style type (colorable weight matching the rest of the book) and instruction text directly beneath the title in a smaller, plain, easy-to-read kid-friendly font. Page number centered at the bottom. 0.75" minimum margin all around, 1"+ inner margin at the binding.
- **Consistency:** recurring characters (the ghost trio, the black cat, the witch, the spider, the vampire bat, the mummy, the scarecrow) keep the same face/body style across every page and across the answer key thumbnails.

## Page-by-Page Briefs

**Page 4 — Maze: "Candy Trail" (Easy)**
A trick-or-treater character (simple child in a pumpkin costume) at the top-left marked with a start arrow; a pile of candy in a bowl at the bottom-right as the goal. One wide, mostly straight path with a single gentle curve, extra-thick corridor, no dead ends. Minimal background decoration (a couple of small leaf shapes) so the path stays the clear visual focus.

**Page 5 — Coloring: "Pretty Pumpkin"**
Single centered jack-o-lantern, same friendly round-pumpkin style used across the series, simple ridge lines dividing the body into 4-5 colorable segments, no background.

**Page 6 — Count and Match: "Count the Bats"**
Three horizontal rows, each containing a small cluster of identical simple bat silhouettes (2, 4, and 6 bats respectively), left-aligned. To the right of the grid, three large numerals (2, 4, 6) presented out of row-order in separate circles, with dotted lines for the child to connect each row to its matching numeral.

**Page 7 — Maze: "Home Sweet Home" (Easy)**
A small ghost character at the top marked with a start arrow; a cute round house with a round door at the bottom as the goal. Single wide path with one gentle S-curve, no branching dead ends. A couple of small stars scattered in the open background space outside the maze corridor.

**Page 8 — I-Spy: "I Spy Halloween!"**
A friendly front-yard scene: a house with a porch, one tree, a low fence, a pathway. Scattered within the scene: 1 black cat (sitting near the porch), 2 bats (flying above the tree), 3 pumpkins (on the porch steps, varying size), 1 spider (on a small web strung between the fence posts). Items sized large enough to spot easily; empty circles printed faintly near each item type is not needed — the child circles directly on the art.

**Page 9 — Dot-to-Dot (1-10): "Connect the Dots"**
Ten large numbered dots arranged in a round pumpkin outline (simple circular silhouette with one small stem bump at the top made from dots 9-10). Numerals large and clearly placed just outside each dot.

**Page 10 — Coloring: "Friendly Witch"**
Single centered witch character bust/portrait (head, hat, shoulders), same friendly-witch style as book 1's witch for series consistency, simple hat band and a few strands of hair as colorable detail.

**Page 11 — Count and Match: "Count the Candy Corn"**
Three horizontal rows of simple candy corn shapes (1, 3, and 5 pieces respectively), matched via dotted lines to numerals 1, 3, 5 printed in circles to the right, presented out of order.

**Page 12 — Maze: "Yarn... I Mean, Pumpkin!" (Easy)**
Black cat character at the start (left side) marked with an arrow; a round pumpkin at the goal (right side). Mostly wide single path with one short branch that dead-ends after a couple of turns — the first gentle introduction to a wrong turn in the book.

**Page 13 — Word Search — Easy**
An 8x8 letter grid in bold blocky monospace-style letters, containing the words BAT, CAT, BOO, OWL, MOON placed only horizontally and vertically (no diagonals, no reversed words). Word list printed in a row beneath the grid, each word in a small rounded box.

**Page 14 — Spot the Difference — Easy**
Two side-by-side haunted-house line-art pictures (same house, same simple yard) with 5 clear differences: (1) one window present/missing, (2) an extra bat in the sky on one side, (3) the jack-o-lantern's face expression different, (4) a tree present/missing a branch, (5) the door shape changed (round vs. arched). Panels separated by a thin vertical divider line.

**Page 15 — Coloring: "Wise Owl"**
Single centered owl on a branch, same owl style as book 1, round face, big circular eyes, simple wing-feather lines, small crescent moon in the background corner.

**Page 16 — Maze: "To the Cauldron!" (Medium)**
Witch character at the start (top) marked with an arrow; a bubbling cauldron at the goal (bottom). Medium-complexity path with 2-3 dead-end branches that loop back on themselves, requiring the child to backtrack. Corridor width still generous.

**Page 17 — Dot-to-Dot (1-15): "Connect the Dots"**
Fifteen large numbered dots forming a bat shape with two spread wings (dots 1-6 outline one wing, 7-10 the body, 11-15 the second wing). Numerals large and clear.

**Page 18 — Count and Match: "Count the Ghosts"**
Three rows of simple ghost silhouettes (3, 5, and 7 ghosts respectively), matched via dotted lines to numerals 3, 5, 7 in circles, presented out of order.

**Page 19 — I-Spy: "I Spy in the Spooky Forest!"**
A friendly forest scene with 3-4 simple trees, fallen leaves, and mushrooms scattered on the ground. Scattered within: 2 owls (one perched, one mid-flight), 3 bats (flying at different heights), 1 black cat (peeking from behind a tree), 4 mushrooms (varying size, spread across the ground). More visually busy than page 8 to reflect the difficulty increase, but still uncluttered enough for clean circling.

**Page 20 — Word Search — Medium**
A 10x10 letter grid containing GHOST, WITCH, SPIDER, CANDY, PUMPKIN, TRICK, placed horizontally and vertically only (still no diagonals at this stage). Word list beneath the grid.

**Page 21 — Coloring: "Haunted House"**
Full-scene haunted house (same friendly crooked-house style as book 1's haunted house page), with a small yard, a leafless tree, and 2 jack-o-lanterns on the porch steps for extra colorable detail.

**Page 22 — Maze: "Web It Up" (Medium)**
Spider character at the start (bottom-left) marked with an arrow; a large round web at the goal (top-right). Winding path with 3 dead-end branches; corridor curves in loose loops to visually echo a web-like structure without sacrificing solvability.

**Page 23 — Spot the Difference — Medium**
Two side-by-side trick-or-treating scenes (3 kids in costume walking up to a porch) with 6 differences: costume pattern/shape changes on one child, an extra pumpkin on one porch, a bat present/missing in the sky, a different candy bucket shape, a window shape changed, a fence rail present/missing.

**Page 24 — Count and Match: "Count the Spiders"**
Three rows of simple round spiders (4, 6, and 8 respectively), matched via dotted lines to numerals 4, 6, 8 in circles, presented out of order.

**Page 25 — Dot-to-Dot (1-20): "Connect the Dots"**
Twenty large numbered dots: dots 1-15 form the round outline of a jack-o-lantern, dots 16-20 form the triangle eyes, nose, and zig-zag mouth as a connected bonus detail sequence inside the main outline.

**Page 26 — Maze: "Treasure Hunt" (Medium-Hard)**
Friendly mummy character at the start (top-left) marked with an arrow; a small round treasure chest at the goal (bottom-right). More winding path with 4 dead-end branches and a couple of switchback turns, corridor width slightly narrower than earlier easy mazes but still comfortably traceable.

**Page 27 — Coloring: "Scarecrow in the Field"**
Full-scene scarecrow (same style as book 1) standing among a small pumpkin patch with 2-3 pumpkins and simple curling vines, ground line with grass tufts.

**Page 28 — Word Search — Medium-Hard**
A 12x12 letter grid containing SKELETON, LANTERN, BROOM, VAMPIRE, MASK, TREAT, MOON, placed horizontally, vertically, and — for the first time in the book — diagonally. Word list beneath the grid, diagonal words visually distinguished with a small arrow icon note ("↘ some words go diagonal!") in the instructions area.

**Page 29 — Count and Match: "Count the Owls"**
Three rows of simple owls (5, 7, and 9 respectively), matched via dotted lines to numerals 5, 7, 9 in circles, presented out of order.

**Page 30 — Maze: "Fly Home, Little Bat" (Hard)**
Vampire bat character at the start (top) marked with an arrow; a cave opening (simple rounded arch shape) at the goal (bottom). Hard maze with 5 dead-end branches, tighter turns, and a longer overall path length than any previous maze in the book, while keeping corridor width consistent and legible.

**Page 31 — Spot the Difference — Hard**
Two side-by-side pictures of a table with a candy bucket and scattered wrapped candies, with 7 smaller/trickier differences: candy count difference, one candy's wrapper twist direction, bucket handle present/missing, a small spider added/removed nearby, a tablecloth pattern line changed, a candle flame shape changed, a shadow line difference.

**Page 32 — Dot-to-Dot (1-20): "Connect the Dots"**
Twenty large numbered dots forming a sitting cat with a curled tail (dots 1-12 form the body and head outline, 13-16 the ears and face features, 17-20 the curled tail).

**Page 33 — I-Spy: "I Spy on Trick-or-Treat Street!"**
The busiest scene in the book: a short street with 3 houses, several porches with jack-o-lanterns, a sidewalk, and a night sky. Scattered within: 3 pumpkins (on different porches), 4 bats (flying at varying heights), 2 witches (one flying, one walking), 1 black cat (on a fence), 5 trick-or-treaters (walking in a loose group along the sidewalk in different costumes). Despite the higher item count, each item stays a clean, separable, easily-circled shape — no overlapping outlines.

**Page 34 — Word Search — Hard**
A 14x14 letter grid containing HALLOWEEN, COSTUME, CAULDRON, GRAVEYARD, SPOOKY, MIDNIGHT, CANDY, WITCH — the longest word list and longest individual words in the book — placed horizontally, vertically, and diagonally (including some reversed diagonals). Word list beneath the grid in two neat columns.

**Page 35 — Count and Match: "Count the Jack-o-Lanterns"**
Three rows of simple jack-o-lanterns with varied faces (6, 8, and 10 respectively — the highest counts in the count-and-match set), matched via dotted lines to numerals 6, 8, 10 in circles, presented out of order.

**Page 36 — Coloring: "Trick-or-Treat Friends"**
Full-scene group of 4-5 kids in varied costumes (ghost, pumpkin, witch, cat, superhero-cape) standing together holding treat bags, the most detailed coloring page in the book, ground line beneath their feet, one moon in the sky.

**Page 37 — Maze: "Graveyard Party" (Hardest)**
Three small ghost characters together at the start (top-left) marked with an arrow; a party scene (bunting, a small table with treats) at the goal (bottom-right). The grand-finale maze: the largest and most winding path in the book with 6 dead-end branches, weaving between 4-5 simple rounded gravestone shapes used as maze wall decoration (plain, friendly-shaped headstones with no text/imagery that reads as scary — some with a small smiling ghost peeking from behind one).

**Page 38 — Word Search — Bonus**
A 14x14 "champion round" grid mixing easy and hard words: OWL, BAT, CAT, WEB, BOO, TRICK, TREAT, GHOST, CANDY, SPOOKY — placed horizontally, vertically, and diagonally. Presented with a small celebratory banner/ribbon graphic above the grid reading "Bonus Puzzle!" to mark it as the final, most rewarding word search.

**Page 39 — Answer Key A (Mazes & Dot-to-Dots)**
A clean grid layout of small thumbnail reproductions (roughly 2" x 2" each) of every maze and dot-to-dot page in the book, in page order. Each maze thumbnail shows the single correct path traced as a bold dashed line from start to finish. Each dot-to-dot thumbnail shows the completed connected shape. Small page-number label beneath each thumbnail.

**Page 40 — Answer Key B (Word Searches)**
A grid layout of small thumbnail reproductions of each word search grid (pages 13, 20, 28, 34, 38) with all found words highlighted via a rounded outline/circle around each word's letters. Word list repeated in small text beneath each thumbnail with each word checked off.

**Page 41 — Answer Key C (Spot the Difference & Count and Match)**
Spot-the-difference thumbnails (pages 14, 23, 31) shown with all differences circled in both panels. Count-and-match thumbnails (pages 6, 11, 18, 24, 29, 35) shown with the correct connecting lines drawn between each row and its numeral.

**Page 42 — "Great Job!" Certificate**
Decorative certificate layout matching the series style: ornamental border of repeating small icons (maze path swirl, pencil, star, pumpkin, ghost) framing the page, large open banner/ribbon shape at the top for the "Great Job!" headline lettering, a blank ruled line in the center for the child's name, small celebratory cluster of Halloween icons at the bottom — rendered as colorable line art.
