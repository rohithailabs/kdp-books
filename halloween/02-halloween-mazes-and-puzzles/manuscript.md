**Page count summary:** 3 front matter pages + 35 activity pages (mazes, coloring, count-and-match, I-Spy, dot-to-dot, word searches, spot-the-difference) + 3 answer key pages + 1 closing certificate = 42 total interior pages.

**Difficulty progression:** Pages 4-15 (easy: wide-path mazes, low dot counts, 5-word searches, 5-difference spot puzzles) → Pages 16-29 (medium: dead-end mazes, 15-dot connect-the-dots, 6-7 word searches with some diagonals, 6-difference spot puzzles) → Pages 30-38 (hard: winding multi-dead-end mazes, 20-dot connect-the-dots, 8-10 word searches with diagonals, 7-difference spot puzzles, busiest I-Spy scene).
