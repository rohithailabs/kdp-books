## Back-Cover / Product Description

**Mazes, puzzles, and Halloween fun — all in one book!**

Halloween Mazes & Puzzles for Kids is packed with 35 spooky-fun activities made just for kids ages 4 to 8: help a trick-or-treater find the candy, guide a lost ghost home, connect the dots to reveal a jack-o-lantern, search for hidden Halloween words, spot the differences between two haunted houses, and count bats, spiders, and pumpkins along the way.

The book starts easy — with wide, simple mazes and short word lists — and gets a little trickier page by page, so it grows right along with your child's skills. Younger kids can trace the early mazes with a finger, while older kids will love tackling the harder mazes, longer word searches, and trickiest spot-the-difference puzzles near the end.

Every puzzle is drawn in bold, friendly, cute-spooky line art (think smiling ghosts and giggling pumpkins, never scary), and a complete answer key at the back makes it easy for grown-ups to check the work — or for kids to check their own once they're ready.

Perfect for classroom Halloween parties, quiet-time activities, road trips, or a not-too-scary Halloween gift, this book gives kids hours of hands-on puzzle fun.

**Inside you'll find:**
- 35 unique activity pages: mazes, dot-to-dots, word searches, spot-the-difference, count-and-match, I-Spy, and a few coloring pages mixed in
- Difficulty that builds gradually from easy to hard
- A full answer key for every puzzle
- A "This Book Belongs To" page and a note for grown-ups
- A "Great Job!" certificate to celebrate finishing the book

Grab a pencil and get ready for a spook-tacular puzzle adventure!
