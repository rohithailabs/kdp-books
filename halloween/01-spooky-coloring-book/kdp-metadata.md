## Back-Cover / Product Description

**Boo! Get ready for the cutest, not-scary Halloween coloring book made just for little hands!**

Spooky Fun is packed with 40 adorable Halloween pictures for your child to color, from friendly ghosts and giggling jack-o-lanterns to witches on broomsticks, black cats, haunted (but cozy!) houses, trick-or-treaters in costume, and a whole parade of candy corn and bats. Every picture is drawn with big, bold, easy-to-color lines — perfect for crayons, markers, or colored pencils, and just right for growing hands ages 4 to 8.

Simple single-picture pages are perfect for your youngest colorists, while a few busier full-scene pictures give older kids more to explore. There's even a "This Book Belongs To" page to make it their very own, and a "Great Job!" certificate at the end to celebrate finishing the whole book.

No scares here — just smiling pumpkins, friendly witches, and cute (never creepy) creatures, making this the perfect Halloween activity for home, the classroom, a Halloween party favor, or a quiet-time treat during the spooky season.

Each page is printed on its own single-sided-friendly layout with plenty of room to color, and the sturdy 8.5" x 8.5" size is easy for little hands to hold. Grab your crayons and get ready for some Spooky Fun this Halloween!
