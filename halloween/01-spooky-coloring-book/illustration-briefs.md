# Spooky Fun: My First Halloween Coloring Book — Illustration Briefs

## Recurring Style Guide (applies to every interior page)

- **Line art only** — pure black outlines on white, no gray fill, no shading, no color. Every enclosed shape must be a large, clean, closable region a child can color without tiny gaps.
- **Line weight:** bold outer contour lines at an equivalent of 3pt; inner detail lines at 2pt. No hairline (under 1pt) details — they clog with crayon and don't reproduce well at KDP's print resolution.
- **Character style:** rounded, chubby "cute-spooky" proportions — big heads, big eyes, soft curves. No fangs dripping blood, no menacing expressions, no gore, no weapons. Ghosts and monsters always smile or look curious/friendly.
- **Complexity scales with page type:** "single object" pages (younger end of 4-8) = one large centered subject, 5-10 colorable regions, generous white space, no background clutter. "Full scene" pages (older end of 4-8) = a subject plus simple environment (ground line, a few background elements, maybe 2-3 secondary characters), 12-25 colorable regions, still with open, uncluttered shapes — never tiny or intricate.
- **Composition:** subject occupies roughly 70-85% of the live print area, centered, sitting on a simple ground line where relevant (no full-bleed complex backgrounds). 0.75" minimum margin all around, with a wider (1"+) inner margin next to the binding.
- **Page furniture:** page title in a simple rounded hand-lettering-style font at the top of the page, matching line weight to the art so it can be colored too. Page number centered at the bottom in a small plain numeral.
- **Consistency:** recurring characters (ghost, black cat, witch, jack-o-lantern) keep the same face style and proportions across every page they appear on, so the book reads as one coherent world.

## Page-by-Page Briefs

**Page 4 — "Boo!" (Friendly Ghost)**
Single object. One round-bodied ghost floating mid-air, arms raised in a cheerful "boo" pose, simple oval eyes and an open smiling mouth, wavy scalloped hem at the bottom. No background elements beyond 2-3 small motion swirl lines. Large open body area for coloring.

**Page 5 — "Smiley Pumpkin"**
Single object. One round jack-o-lantern centered, big triangle eyes, triangle nose, wide zig-zag smile with 2-3 teeth, a curled stem and one leaf on top. Vertical pumpkin ridge lines divide the body into 4-5 large colorable segments.

**Page 6 — "Witch in the Sky" (full scene)**
Witch with a pointed hat, simple flowing dress/cape, sitting sideways on a broomstick, flying left-to-right across the page. Crescent moon in the upper corner, 3-4 small stars, one small cloud. Witch's face is friendly, rounded, smiling — no warts, no green skin implied (leave skin tone open for the colorist).

**Page 7 — "Black Cat"**
Single object. One cat sitting upright facing forward, tail curled around its front paws, triangle ears, round eyes, small closed smiling mouth, a few simple whisker lines. Body divided by a subtle chest-patch outline for a two-tone coloring option.

**Page 8 — "Haunted House on the Hill" (full scene)**
A crooked-but-cute two-story house with a few off-kilter windows (round or arched, friendly shapes, no broken glass), a short picket fence in front, one bare tree with round leaf clusters, sitting on a gently curved hill line. Small bat silhouette in the sky optional.

**Page 9 — "Roar! Trick-or-Treat!"**
Single object. Child standing in a soft dinosaur costume (rounded spine bumps, hood with dino face on head, tail behind), holding a round trick-or-treat bag in one hand. Simple standing pose, face visible and smiling inside the hood.

**Page 10 — "Candy Corn Trio"**
Single object, very simple. Three candy corn shapes side by side, each with three horizontal bands (white tip, orange middle, yellow base) outlined for easy separate coloring. Great low-detail page for the youngest colorists.

**Page 11 — "Upside-Down Bat"**
Single object. One round, chubby bat hanging by its feet from a simple tree branch at the top of the page, wings folded/wrapped loosely around its body, big round friendly eyes open, small smile.

**Page 12 — "Itsy Bitsy Spider"**
Single object. One round-bodied spider with 8 simple curved legs sitting in the center of a large round web (web drawn as a few straight spokes crossed with 2-3 concentric wavy rings). Big cute eyes on the spider, no fangs.

**Page 13 — "Night Owl"**
Single object. Owl perched on a tree branch, large round face with big circular eyes, small triangle beak, wing detail as simple curved feather lines, tail feathers fanned. Small crescent moon behind/above the owl.

**Page 14 — "Scarecrow's Pumpkin Patch" (full scene)**
Scarecrow with a round stuffed head, button eyes, stitched smile, straw poking from sleeves/cuffs, standing on a wooden cross-post in the center. Three pumpkins of varying size scattered around its feet, a few simple curling vine lines connecting them, ground line with 2-3 small grass tufts.

**Page 15 — "Ghost Family Trick-or-Treating" (full scene)**
Three ghosts of graduated sizes (tall, medium, small) standing side by side holding hands or standing close together, each holding a small trick-or-treat bag in their free hand, simple ground line beneath them, one or two small stars in the sky.

**Page 16 — "Pick a Pumpkin" (full scene)**
A pumpkin patch scene: one small, one medium, one large pumpkin arranged in the foreground with simple curling vines connecting them, a low fence or a few leafy vine clusters in the background, ground line.

**Page 17 — "Bubble, Bubble" (full scene)**
A round cauldron on three short legs sitting over a simple fire-swirl shape, with a friendly black cat standing beside it holding a long spoon (or paw resting on the rim), a few round bubble shapes rising from the pot.

**Page 18 — "Knock, Knock!" (full scene)**
Three kids in simple costumes (one ghost sheet, one pumpkin costume, one cat-ears-and-tail) standing in front of a friendly round door with a jack-o-lantern beside it, all holding treat bags, ground line, small porch step.

**Page 19 — "Bat Family Flying Home" (full scene)**
Three bats of graduated sizes flying together in a loose diagonal line across the page, wings spread, one large full moon behind them, 3-4 small stars.

**Page 20 — "Web Between the Pumpkins"**
Single object/decorative. Two small round pumpkins on the ground with a spider web strung between their stems, web drawn with straight spokes and wavy connecting rings, one tiny spider sitting in a corner of the web.

**Page 21 — "Jack-o-Lantern Porch" (full scene)**
Front porch of a house (simple door, one window, roofline) with three steps leading up, one jack-o-lantern with a different face on each step, a small welcome mat shape at the bottom.

**Page 22 — "Little Ghost Says Hi"**
Single object. Child wearing a simple ghost-sheet costume with two round eye holes, standing and knocking with one fist raised toward an implied door edge at the page's side, treat bag in the other hand.

**Page 23 — "Full Candy Bucket"**
Single object. A round trick-or-treat bucket (jack-o-lantern face optional on the bucket) filled with an overflowing pile of individually-outlined wrapped candies (simple twist-wrapper shapes) spilling slightly over the rim.

**Page 24 — "Witch's Hat and Broom"**
Single object/decorative. A classic pointed witch hat with a wide brim and a small buckle band, crossed diagonally over a broomstick with simple straw bristle lines at the base. A tiny bow or star can sit on the hat band.

**Page 25 — "Friendly Mummy"**
Single object. Round-bodied mummy character wrapped in overlapping bandage strips (drawn as simple curved bands, not tangled), one arm raised in a wave, two big round eyes peeking out between wraps, big smile.

**Page 26 — "Silly Vampire Bat"**
Single object. Cartoon bat facing forward, wings spread wide, big round eyes, an open smiling mouth showing two small rounded (not sharp) fang tips — kept playful, not scary.

**Page 27 — "Pumpkin Parade" (full scene)**
Five jack-o-lanterns in a row, each a similar round base shape but with a distinct funny face (surprised, winking, laughing, sleepy, tongue-out), sitting on a simple ground line, evenly spaced.

**Page 28 — "Wise Old Owl"**
Single object. Owl standing/perched, wearing a small witch hat tilted on its head, same round-face owl style as page 13 for consistency, wing tips visible at sides.

**Page 29 — "Bobbing for Apples" (full scene)**
Large round tub/barrel filled with 4-5 round apples floating at the water line (drawn as a simple wavy horizontal line inside the tub), two kids leaning over opposite sides of the tub with hands on the rim, simple background party bunting line optional at the top.

**Page 30 — "Cat on a Pumpkin"**
Single object. Same black cat character as page 7, now sitting on top of a large round pumpkin, tail hanging down the side, pumpkin has simple ridge lines and a small stem.

**Page 31 — "Peek-a-Boo Ghost"**
Single object. A round pumpkin in the foreground with a small ghost character peeking out from directly behind it (only the ghost's head and top of body visible over the pumpkin), both with friendly expressions.

**Page 32 — "Scarecrow in the Wind" (full scene)**
Same scarecrow style as page 14, now standing in a cornfield with 4-5 simple tall corn-stalk shapes around it, a few curved motion lines suggesting wind and a couple of leaf shapes blowing sideways.

**Page 33 — "Brave Little Lion"**
Single object. Child in a soft lion costume (round mane made of simple loop shapes around the hood, tail with a tuft at the end), standing and holding up a full, round treat bag proudly.

**Page 34 — "Spooky Tree" (full scene)**
One large bare tree with curved, friendly (not gnarled/scary) branches, an owl (same style as page 13) perched on one branch, two small bats flying near the top, a crescent moon in the sky behind the tree.

**Page 35 — "Witch and the Castle" (full scene)**
Same witch/broomstick character as page 6, flying past a small round-towered castle in the background (simple turret shapes, one round window), a few stars and a crescent moon filling the remaining sky space.

**Page 36 — "Party Pumpkin"**
Single object. A round jack-o-lantern with a cheerful face wearing a small triangular party hat with a pompom on top, one or two simple confetti-dot shapes floating nearby.

**Page 37 — "Moonlight Parade" (full scene)**
A group of 4-5 kids in different simple costumes (ghost, pumpkin, cat, witch, superhero-cape) walking together in a loose row, treat bags in hand, one large moon overhead, ground line beneath their feet.

**Page 38 — "Spider with a Bow"**
Single object. Same round spider style as page 12, now with a simple bow shape on top of its head between two of its front legs, sitting alone (no web needed) for a simpler, younger-friendly page.

**Page 39 — "Bats Around the House" (full scene)**
Same haunted house style as page 8 (silhouette outline only, no fill), with 4-5 bats of varying size circling above and around the roofline in a loose swirl pattern.

**Page 40 — "Cauldron Friends" (full scene)**
Same cauldron as page 17, now with a small bat perched on one side of the rim and a small spider peeking over the other side, both looking down into the pot, a few round bubble shapes rising above.

**Page 41 — "Starry Halloween Night" (full scene)**
Busy night-sky scene: 3-4 bats mid-flight at varying heights, one owl perched on a small branch in a corner, 6-8 star shapes scattered across the sky, and one glowing jack-o-lantern sitting on the ground at the bottom of the page. This is one of the two most detailed pages in the book — appropriate for the top of the 4-8 age range.

**Page 42 — "Trick-or-Treat Sign"**
Decorative/text page. Large bubble-letter word art spelling "TRICK OR TREAT" across the center of the page (letters outlined thick enough to color in, like the rest of the book), framed by a repeating border pattern alternating small pumpkins and candy corn shapes around all four edges.

**Page 43 — "Happy Halloween!" (full scene, grand finale)**
The most detailed page in the book. A wide banner reading "HAPPY HALLOWEEN" strung between two posts (or held up by two bats) across the top third of the page. Below the banner: a small crowd of celebrating characters — 2 ghosts, 2 jack-o-lanterns with faces, 1 black cat, and 1 witch — arranged in a loose group, arms/branches raised as if celebrating, confetti-dot shapes scattered throughout, ground line beneath.

**Page 44 — "Great Job!" Certificate**
Decorative certificate layout. Ornamental border framing the page made of a repeating small-icon pattern (ghost, pumpkin, bat, cat, candy corn rotating around the edge). Large open banner/ribbon shape at the top for the "Great Job!" headline lettering, a blank ruled line in the center for the child's name, and a small cluster of celebratory Halloween icons (jack-o-lantern, ghost, bat) at the bottom — all rendered as colorable line art so the certificate itself can be colored before display.
