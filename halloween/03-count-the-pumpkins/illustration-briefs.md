# Count the Pumpkins: A Halloween Counting & Coloring Book — Illustration Briefs

## Recurring Style Guide (applies to every interior page)

- **Line art only** — pure black outlines on white, no gray fill, no shading, no color.
- **Line weight:** bold outer contour lines at an equivalent of 3pt; inner detail lines at 2pt; numeral tracing strokes at a thick, evenly-weighted 4pt dotted/dashed line so tracing is easy for small hands.
- **Character style:** same rounded, chubby "cute-spooky" proportions as the rest of the catalog — big heads, big friendly eyes, soft curves, always smiling or curious, never scary.
- **Standard number-page layout (pages 4-23):** a consistent three-zone template on every page —
  1. **Top zone:** large dotted tracing numeral (matching the page's number) on the left, paired with the number word spelled out in dotted tracing letters on the right (e.g., "1" beside "one").
  2. **Middle zone:** the themed picture containing the exact quantity of objects for that page, arranged in a clear, evenly-spaced, non-overlapping layout (a simple grid, row, or loose scatter depending on the object) so every single object is easy to count individually. Objects for one number are always the same character/object repeated at consistent size — no size variation that could suggest a different count.
  3. **Bottom zone:** a row of small empty square tracing/checkboxes, one per object in that page's count, for the child to check off while counting.
- **Object-count accuracy is critical:** the illustrator must render the EXACT number of objects stated in the page text — this is a counting book, so miscounts break the activity. Double-check each page's rendered count against the manuscript before finalizing art.
- **Composition:** template zones stay in consistent proportion across all 20 number pages so the book has a strong, predictable rhythm. 0.75" minimum margin all around, 1"+ inner margin at the binding. Page number centered at the bottom, below the checkbox row.
- **Consistency:** each recurring object/character (pumpkin, ghost, bat, cat, spider, owl, witch hat, candy, etc.) keeps the same simple design every time it appears, including on the review pages.

**Page 24 — Bonus Review: "Count to 20!"**
A number line/ladder strip across the top of the page showing numerals 1 through 20 in sequence inside small connected boxes, sized for a child's finger to point along while counting aloud. Below it, one large pumpkin-patch scene containing exactly 20 pumpkins of a single consistent size, arranged in a naturalistic scattered-but-countable layout (e.g., loosely gridded rows with slight offsets) with a few simple vine lines connecting them.

**Page 25 — Bonus Tracing: "Trace Every Number!"**
A clean 4-column x 5-row grid containing all numerals 1 through 20 in large, evenly-sized dotted tracing font, one numeral per cell. Beside each numeral, a tiny decorative Halloween icon (rotating through pumpkin, ghost, bat, and cat every few cells) sized small enough not to compete with the tracing numeral itself.

**Page 26 — "Great Job!" Certificate**
Decorative certificate layout. Ornamental border made of ten small numbered pumpkins (numbered 1 through 10, alternating around the visible edge) framing the page. Large open banner/ribbon shape at the top for the "Great Job!" headline lettering, a blank ruled line in the center for the child's name, and a small celebratory cluster of Halloween icons (jack-o-lantern, ghost, bat, numeral "20") at the bottom — all rendered as colorable line art.
