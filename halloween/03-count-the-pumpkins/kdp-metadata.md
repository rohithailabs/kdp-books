## Back-Cover / Product Description

**Learn to count from 1 to 20 with a pumpkin patch full of Halloween friends!**

Count the Pumpkins is a fun, hands-on counting and coloring book made for kids ages 4 to 8. Each page focuses on one number, from 1 all the way to 20 — trace the big numeral, say the number word, count a group of Halloween pictures (pumpkins, ghosts, bats, black cats, spiders, candy, and more), check off each one as you count, and then color the whole picture in!

The book builds skills step by step: little ones can start with the easy early numbers (1-10), while older kids can stretch their counting all the way to 20 with trickier pages like counting mummy bandage wraps, haunted house windows, and a grand finale Halloween parade of twenty characters. Two bonus pages at the end offer a full 1-20 number-line review and a big tracing practice page for extra number-writing fun.

With bold, friendly "cute-spooky" line art — smiling pumpkins, cheerful ghosts, and not-scary creatures throughout — this book is perfect for preschool and kindergarten learning at home, in the classroom, or as a not-too-spooky Halloween gift.

**Inside you'll find:**
- 20 number pages covering 1 through 20, each with tracing, counting, and coloring
- Consistent, easy-to-follow layout on every page: trace the number, count the picture, color it in
- 2 bonus pages: a 1-20 number line review and a full numeral tracing practice page
- A "This Book Belongs To" page and a note for grown-ups
- A "Great Job!" certificate to celebrate finishing the book

Grab your crayons and count your way through a Halloween full of pumpkins, ghosts, and friendly counting fun!
