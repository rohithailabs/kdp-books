### Back-Cover / Product Description
Get ready to count down to a brand-new year with **New Year Countdown: Puzzles & Games for Kids**! This 30-page puzzle-and-activity book takes kids ages 4 to 8 on a real countdown — from 10 all the way down to 1 — with a brain-teasing challenge waiting at every number.

Along the way, kids will race through mazes, connect the dots to reveal party hats, clocks, and fireworks, search for hidden New Year words, spot the differences between two silly pictures, match party hats and noisemakers, and go on seek-and-find missions through busy countdown-party scenes. The puzzles start big, simple, and friendly for little beginners — and get a little trickier with every number, so older kids stay challenged all the way to "1... Happy New Year!"

After the countdown hits midnight, two bonus coloring pages give hands a well-earned rest before a "Great Job!" certificate to fill out and show off. A complete answer key in the back lets parents check every maze, dot-to-dot, word search, and spot-the-difference at a glance.

With clear, kindergarten-level instructions on every page, this book is perfect for solo puzzlers and read-aloud time alike. It's an ideal screen-free activity for New Year's Eve parties, car rides, classroom countdowns, or quiet mornings in — a puzzle book that grows with your child instead of a one-note coloring book.

Bold black-and-white line art keeps every puzzle crisp and clear, and makes this a budget-friendly gift the whole family can enjoy together.
