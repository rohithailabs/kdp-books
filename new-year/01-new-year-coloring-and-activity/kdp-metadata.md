### Back-Cover / Product Description
Ring in the New Year with **Sparkle & Shine** — a joyful coloring and activity book made just for little celebrators ages 4 to 8!

Inside, kids will find 32 pages packed with big, bold, easy-to-color pictures: dazzling fireworks bursting over the skyline, party hats and confetti, a giant midnight clock, balloons galore, silly animals in party hats, and a very special Baby New Year. Along the way, your child can count balloons and stars, match party hats to their shadows, help a firework find its way through a friendly maze, and fill in a sweet "My New Year Wishes" page where they draw their very own hopes and dreams for the year ahead.

Every page uses bold, simple line art designed for small hands — perfect for crayons, markers, or colored pencils. Clear instructions are written at a kindergarten reading level, so grown-ups can read along with pre-readers, and early readers can go it alone. The book opens with a spot for your child's name and a New Year wish, and finishes with a colorful "Great Job!" certificate they'll be proud to hang up.

Whether you're starting a new family tradition, keeping little hands busy on New Year's Eve, or just looking for a cheerful, screen-free activity, Sparkle & Shine is the perfect way to countdown to a brand new year — together.

A wonderful stocking stuffer, party favor, classroom gift, or New Year's Eve activity for toddlers and young children.
