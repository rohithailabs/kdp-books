# Sparkle & Shine: My First New Year Coloring & Activity Book
## Illustration Briefs

### Recurring Style Guide (applies to every interior page unless noted)
- **Line art only:** pure black outlines on white, no gray fill, no shading, no color. Every enclosed shape must be a clean, closed outline so a young child can color inside it without the ink bleeding outward.
- **Line weight:** bold outer contours at roughly 2.5-3pt equivalent; interior detail lines slightly thinner (1.5-2pt) so the page doesn't look cluttered when printed small.
- **Detail level:** minimal-to-moderate. Favor big, simple, huggable shapes (rounded balloons, chubby animals, soft firework bursts) over fine cross-hatching or tiny repeating patterns — ages 4-8 need generously sized open areas to color.
- **Composition:** one subject per page, centered with balanced negative space; full-scene pages use a simple foreground/midground/background layering (max 3 layers) so it still reads clearly at coloring-book scale.
- **Margins:** minimum 0.5" inner (gutter) margin and 0.4" outer margins on every page to survive perfect-binding trim.
- **Page number:** small, simple numeral centered at the bottom of every interior page, outside the illustration frame.
- **Title/instruction lettering:** hand-drawn-style bold rounded lettering (easy to render in a kid-friendly display font at layout stage), placed at the top of the page above the illustration, never overlapping the coloring area.
- **Tone:** cheerful, warm, welcoming — no scary imagery, no alcohol, no nightclub/party-adult imagery. Confetti, balloons, fireworks, party hats, clocks, calendars, and friendly animals only.
- **Firework style:** drawn as simple radiating burst shapes (a center dot with 6-10 straight or gently curved rays ending in small circles/stars) — easy to color, never realistic/photographic.
- **Answer key art (page 32 only):** small simplified thumbnail versions of the relevant activity pages with the solution overlaid as a dashed line or checkmark; thumbnails may be roughly 1/4 page size.

### Page 1 — Title Page
Large centered title lettering "Sparkle & Shine" with subtitle "My First New Year Coloring & Activity Book" beneath it in smaller lettering. Surround with a loose ring of simple line-art elements: two firework bursts (upper corners), one party hat (lower left), a cluster of three balloons (lower right), and light scattered confetti dot/star shapes filling empty space. Keep the center third of the page clear so title text doesn't collide with art.

### Page 2 — This Book Belongs To
Decorative rectangular frame built from a rope of bunting flags along the top and a row of small balloons along the bottom. Inside the frame: "This Book Belongs To:" with a ruled blank line, and below it "My New Year wish for this year is:" with a second ruled blank line. Corners of the frame get small star/confetti accents.

### Page 3 — Parent/Caregiver Note
Plain text layout (no coloring area). Thin single-line border around the page with a small confetti sprinkle motif only in the four corners — kept light so it doesn't compete with the reading text. Title "A Note for Grown-Ups" set in the same display lettering style as the title page, smaller.

### Page 4 — Sparkle, Sparkle! (full scene)
Wide night skyline: simple flat rooftops/building silhouettes along the bottom third (drawn as clean outlines, not filled black), a large open sky above. Three firework bursts of varying size at different heights, each with clearly separated coloring zones between rays. A thin crescent moon outline in one upper corner.

### Page 5 — My Party Hat (single object)
One large classic cone-shaped party hat centered, chin elastic shown as a simple curved line, a big pom-pom circle on top, and 3-4 simple stripe or zigzag pattern lines down the cone (as coloring guide lines, not filled).

### Page 6 — Confetti and Balloons (single object/cluster)
A bunch of 5 round balloons of graduated size tied together with curling ribbon lines, floating confetti shapes (small circles, triangles, and short rectangles) scattered loosely across the open background.

### Page 7 — Almost Midnight! (full scene)
Large round clock face filling most of the page, simple numerals 1-12 around the rim, hour and minute hands pointing just before 12. Small star and sparkle-line accents radiating softly from the top of the clock. Optional small mantle/shelf line beneath the clock to ground it.

### Page 8 — Count and Color the Balloons (activity)
Seven balloons in a single loose horizontal row, varied sizes so counting feels natural, each with a simple string curling down. Small empty rectangle box beneath the row for the child to write a number. Keep balloons well-spaced (no overlap) so each is easy to count and color individually.

### Page 9 — Noisy and Fun! (single object)
One large curly paper noisemaker horn (party blower) shown extended, spiral body with a fringed paper cone at the tip, small "TOOT!" motion lines beside the opening.

### Page 10 — Hello, New Year! (full scene)
Banner strung between two simple poles/posts, banner reading "HAPPY NEW YEAR" across triangle bunting flags. Balloons tied to the base of each pole, light confetti sprinkled across the sky.

### Page 11 — Puppy's Party Hat (single object)
One seated puppy (floppy ears, simple round body) wearing a party hat with elastic strap and a small bow-tie collar. Simple ground line beneath.

### Page 12 — Kitten and Confetti (single object)
One kitten in a playful pose, one paw raised batting at a falling confetti piece, sitting on a round cushion. A few confetti shapes drawn mid-fall around it.

### Page 13 — Match the Hats! (activity)
Left column: three party hats, each with a distinct simple pattern — horizontal stripes, five-point stars, polka dots. Right column (shuffled vertical order): three solid black silhouette hat shapes matching those same three outlines. Leave a wide blank middle zone for the child to draw connecting lines. Number each hat lightly (1, 2, 3) and each shadow (A, B, C) in small corner tags to aid matching without giving away the answer.

### Page 14 — Fireworks by the Sea (full scene)
Simple horizon line with gentle wave-crest lines below (2-3 rows) and open sky above. Three firework bursts overhead, one burst's reflection sketched as a loose mirrored outline on the water. A single sailboat silhouette optional in the distance for depth.

### Page 15 — Sleepy Owl, Wide Awake (single object)
One large owl perched on a tree branch, big round eyes wide open, wearing a small party hat tilted on its head. Crescent moon and 2-3 small stars in the background sky.

### Page 16 — Countdown to Midnight! (activity)
Numbers 10 through 1 rendered as large bold dotted/outline numerals for tracing, arranged along a gentle curving path (like stepping stones) from bottom-left to top-right. A small firework burst icon caps the path after the "1" as the reward image. Each numeral sized large enough for a 4-6 year old's pencil control.

### Page 17 — Bear's Big Noisemaker (single object)
One bear cub standing upright on hind legs, blowing into a party horn (small spiral shape at the horn's tip), a few confetti bits drawn near its paws.

### Page 18 — Watching the Sky (full scene)
Backyard scene: a blanket on the ground in the foreground with two children (seated, simple rounded shapes, one pointing up at the sky) and a small dog beside them. String lights strung across the top of the page (a scalloped line with small bulb circles). Three firework bursts fill the upper sky.

### Page 19 — Firework to the Sky! (maze)
Maze walls drawn as clean bold double-lines (consistent ~0.25" corridor width) forming one unambiguous solvable path with 2-3 gentle turns — easy difficulty, no false branches that dead-end more than once. A small rocket/firework icon marks the start at the bottom of the maze; a burst-shape "finish" icon marks the top. Keep wall lines thick and simple so a 4-year-old can trace with a finger before using a pencil.

### Page 20 — Glittery Star (single object)
One large five-point star centered, with 6-8 short sparkle lines radiating from its points/edges as a coloring accent (each sparkle line is its own small closed shape, not just a stray line, so it can be colored too).

### Page 21 — Toot the Horn! (single object)
One small striped kazoo-style party horn with a star-shaped flared end, simple diagonal stripe guide lines down the body.

### Page 22 — Animal Parade (full scene)
Five animals in profile, walking left to right in a loose line: elephant, rabbit, fox, duck, pig — each wearing a simple party hat and holding a balloon string in its trunk/paw/wing. Light ground line beneath the row; a few confetti shapes scattered above.

### Page 23 — My New Year Wishes (activity)
Page divided into four equal quadrant boxes with rounded corners, each with a thin outline border. Small icon + label at the top of each box: a book icon ("Something new I want to learn"), a heart icon ("Someone I love"), a map-pin icon ("Somewhere I want to go"), a sun icon ("Something kind I will do"). Interiors of the boxes left fully blank for free drawing.

### Page 24 — Balloon Bunch (full scene)
A dozen balloons of mixed shapes (round, star-shaped, heart-shaped) clustered together and tied with a single ribbon bow at the bottom, ribbon tails curling loosely. Balloons overlap only slightly at edges so each keeps a distinct enclosed coloring area.

### Page 25 — Pop! Confetti Cannon (full scene)
A simple cone-shaped confetti cannon at the bottom of the page, angled upward, with a wide spray of confetti shapes (circles, stars, short streamers) exploding outward to fill the upper two-thirds of the page.

### Page 26 — Bunny's New Glasses (single object)
One bunny (simple round body, long ears) wearing oversized novelty glasses shaped like the numerals "2027" across the eyes, small bow tie at the neck.

### Page 27 — Fireworks Over the Mountains (full scene)
Layered mountain silhouette range across the lower third (2-3 overlapping peak shapes, outline only), a small row of simple triangle pine trees in the foreground. Two to three large firework bursts fill the sky above the peaks.

### Page 28 — Count and Color the Stars (activity)
Nine stars scattered loosely across the page at varied sizes and rotations (not in a grid, but not overlapping), small empty box at the bottom for the number.

### Page 29 — Baby New Year (full scene)
Baby New Year mascot: a cheerful toddler-proportioned character in a simple diaper/onesie, wearing an oversized top hat, with a sash across the chest reading "2027," holding a small flag on a stick in one hand. Confetti shapes scattered around the figure.

### Page 30 — Turning the Page (full scene)
Large desk calendar block shown at an angle, front page reading "JAN 1" facing forward, previous page reading "DEC 31" shown mid-curl/flip in the upper corner as if turning away. Small sparkle accents around the calendar to suggest a magical moment.

### Page 31 — Great Job! (certificate)
Formal certificate framing: a decorative rectangular border made of small repeating balloon or star shapes, a ribbon-medal graphic in one corner. Centered text: "Great Job! This certifies that ______ colored and celebrated their way through the whole book! Happy New Year!" with the blank as a ruled line.

### Page 32 — Answer Key
Two small thumbnail panels. Top panel: a reduced version of page 13's matching activity with dashed connector lines drawn between each hat and its correct shadow. Bottom panel: a reduced version of page 19's maze with the correct path traced as a dashed line from start to finish. Small captions above each thumbnail: "Page 13" and "Page 19."
