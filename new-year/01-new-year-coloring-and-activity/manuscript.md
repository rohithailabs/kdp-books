# Sparkle & Shine: My First New Year Coloring & Activity Book
## Manuscript — Page-by-Page Content Plan

Trim size: 8.5" x 8.5" | Interior: black line art only | 32 interior pages | Ages 4-8

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title Page | **Sparkle & Shine**<br>*My First New Year Coloring & Activity Book* | Large decorative title lettering surrounded by simple fireworks burst outlines and a party hat. No color — line art only, meant to also be colored in if desired. |

Grab your crayons and ring in the New Year together! | Plain text page, small decorative confetti border, no coloring area needed. |
